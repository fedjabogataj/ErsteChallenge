Task 2: Combining forecasts

Situation
You have received the daily forecasts of 15 different models for the same set of stocks. The models differ.
Try to combine the predictions so that the aggregate is better than the individual models. The stocks are
anonymised, and price data is provided only for the development period.

Data
dev.parquet             for each day and stock, the 15 model forecasts (m01..m15). A forecast made on day t is
                        about the stock's return over the next 10 trading days, from the open of day t+1 to the
                        open of day t+11.
price.parquet           the open price (same currency) for exactly the days and stocks of dev.parquet. You can assume no fees for
                        trading.
test.parquet            the same forecasts for the later period, starting the day after dev.parquet ends.
submission-example.txt  an example submission for the test period; its header lists the submission rules.
The day index is already in trading days, starting from 1.

Your task
Combine the 15 forecasts into one daily allocation for the test period (see submission-example.txt).
Explain how you approached the task, what your solution is and why, and what you can say about the
individual models.

Rules
Any method is fine, whether statistics, ML or anything else. Use only the data provided, and only
information that would have been available at the time. Do not assume anything not visibly stated here.
Any tools are allowed, including AI assistants. You are responsible for the output, so prefer what you
understand. You have ~24 hours. Before your interview timeslot send us the submission file or a link to it.

How it is judged
The most important thing is to see how you approached the challenge and how you think about it.
The submission is judged by a few reasonable metrics on the test period, but the performance is only
secondary and much less important.

Good luck!
